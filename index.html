Game Over

Drag a tray piece (bottom row) onto the grid. Filling a row/column clears it and scatters 1-3 colored "shards" back onto the board via the shard queue (top row of slots) — shard color matches the piece that triggered the clear. If the queue fills up, the next shard force-inserts straight into your active tray instead of waiting. No timer. Game over when nothing in the tray fits anywhere on the board.
